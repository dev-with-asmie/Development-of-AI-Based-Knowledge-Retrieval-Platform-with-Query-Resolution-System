
ACADEMIC POLICY AND UNIVERSITY ADMINISTRATION

Attendance Policy:
Students are expected to maintain at least 75 percent attendance in each subject to be eligible for semester examinations. Attendance is calculated using the total number of classes conducted and the number of classes attended.

Examination Policy:
A semester examination consists of internal assessment and end-semester examination. Internal assessment may include assignments, class tests, and practical evaluations. Students must satisfy the minimum passing requirements in both components according to the applicable academic regulations.

Library Services:
Students can borrow up to three books at a time. The standard borrowing period is fourteen days. Books may be renewed if no other student has reserved them. Late returns may result in a library fine.

Academic Records:
Students can request transcripts and academic records through the academic office. Requests should include the student's registration details and the specific documents required.

Course Registration:
Students must complete course registration before the announced deadline. Late registration requires approval from the academic department.
